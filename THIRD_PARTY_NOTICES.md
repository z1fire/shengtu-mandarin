# Third-party data notices

## CC-CEDICT

English vocabulary glosses for the expanded HSK 2–9 library are derived from
[CC-CEDICT](https://cc-cedict.org/editor/editor.php?handler=Download), a
community-maintained Chinese–English dictionary published by MDBG.

CC-CEDICT is licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Changes made for Shēngtú include selecting and shortening glosses, removing
classifier-only notes, and matching entries to the official HSK syllabus.

The official Mandarin words, pinyin, levels, characters, and grammar inventory
come from the Chinese Tests Service HSK syllabus and are not sourced from
CC-CEDICT.
